Account and Data Deletion

You can delete your Regl & Gebelik Takvimi (developer: Yusuf Koşar) account and your data in two ways: from inside the app, or by sending us an email. You do not need to have the app installed.

1. Deleting from the app

There are two separate delete actions in the app, and they delete different things.

"Delete account" — deletes your account and your cloud backup

Where: Settings → Account → Delete account

What is deleted:

  • Your Firebase account (including your email address) is permanently deleted.
  • Your cloud backup — your period records, cycle settings, pregnancy details, avatar and reminder preferences — is permanently deleted.

This screen also offers "Delete my records on this device too". It is off by default: if you leave it off, the records on your phone stay and you can carry on using the app without an account. If you turn it on, the data on your phone is deleted as well.

You are asked for your password, for security. The deletion removes the cloud backup first, then the account.

"Delete all my data" — deletes everything on this phone only

Where: Settings → Data deletion → Delete all my data

What is deleted:

  • Your period records, cycle settings, pregnancy details, avatar and reminder preferences are deleted from this phone.
  • Scheduled reminders are cancelled and the home screen widget is cleared.
  • Sync preferences and this device's technical records are deleted.
  • If you are signed in, you are signed out and the app starts over.

This action does not touch your cloud backup or your account. If you want to delete your account as well, use the "Delete account" action above.

2. Requesting deletion by email

You can ask for your account to be deleted without installing the app, or when you cannot get to your phone.

Write to: regltakvimi.destek@gmail.com

Please include in your email:

  • In the subject line: Account deletion request
  • The email address you used when creating your account in the app. Sending the request from that address is the easiest way to confirm that the account is yours.
  • Whether you want only your cloud backup deleted, or your whole account.

Once we have your request we act on it and reply to you within 30 days at the latest. Where we cannot confirm that the account is yours we may ask for further information; this is to prevent somebody else's account being deleted.

Only you can reach the records on your phone; it is not possible for us to delete them. To delete the data on your device, use the "Delete all my data" action in the app, or uninstall the app.

What is deleted, what is kept

Data After deletion
Email address (Firebase account) Deleted.
Cloud backup: period records, cycle settings, pregnancy details, avatar, reminder preferences, daily records Deleted.
The records on your phone Deleted if you turned on "Delete my records on this device too", or if you used "Delete all my data"; otherwise they stay on your phone.
Backups of the backup, and server logs Short-lived technical copies may exist in Google's infrastructure; these are deleted by themselves within Google's own retention periods and are not read or used by us.
Correspondence about the deletion request So that we can show the request was carried out, the email correspondence is kept for at most 1 year, then deleted.

Beyond this there is no data left with us after a deletion. No marketing list, no analytics record and no profile is kept — none is ever created.

If you change phones

This app is excluded from Android's automatic backup: your data is not copied to Google Drive. The cost of that is that when you move to a new phone, or delete and reinstall the app, your records will not come back by themselves.

If you want to move your history, take a backup to your account from inside the app before you change phones: sign in from the Account screen and use the "Create backup" button. When you sign in to the same account on the new phone you can restore your backup.

If you have no account you have no backup: records deleted from the phone cannot be brought back.

Is there anything you need to do before deleting

No. The app has no paid version, no subscription and no in-app purchases; there is nothing for you to cancel.
