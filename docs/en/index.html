Regl & Gebelik Takvimi

An Android app that helps you keep track of your cycle and your pregnancy. Your records are kept on your phone and leave the device only for your own account: when you take a backup, when you sync, or when you turn automatic sync on. Nothing is sent while the app is closed.

The app is not a substitute for medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, and it is not a method of contraception. If you have any concern about your health, speak to a healthcare professional. In an emergency, contact your local emergency services.
