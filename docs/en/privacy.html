Privacy Policy

In short: your health data stays on your phone, and leaves the device only for your own account — when you take a backup, when you sync, or when you turn automatic sync on. Automatic sync is off by default, and nothing is sent while the app is closed. No ads, no analytics, no tracking; your data is not sold.

Where your data is kept

Your period records, cycle settings, pregnancy details, avatar choices and reminder preferences are kept on your phone only, by default, in the app's own database. The whole app works without an account.

This data leaves the device for exactly one destination: your own Firebase account. It is sent nowhere else.

If you turn on the app lock, your PIN itself is never stored: only a hash derived from it and a random salt, held in the phone's own secure key store. That information is sent nowhere and is not synced with your account — the lock, like the theme, is a preference belonging to that phone.

The app lock covers the app's screen; it does not encrypt the files on your phone. The real protection on your device is the phone's own screen lock and Android's own encryption.

Reminder notifications and the lock screen

Android shows a notification's title and text on the lock screen, and apps cannot turn that off. The setting that appears to do so (the notification channel's lock screen visibility) is silently ignored by Android; this was measured and confirmed in this app. So while you have a reminder on, its text can be read even when your phone is locked.

The only thing the app can change is the text itself. When Settings > Notifications > "Don't show details in notifications" is on, reminders do not mention periods or pregnancy; they say only that you have a reminder and that you can open the app. Setting up the app lock turns this on too, because somebody who set up a lock wanted exactly this; you can turn it back off in the same place.

This setting stays on your phone and is not synced with your account: who sees your lock screen depends on the phone in your hand and the people around you. The app's icon still appears on the notification — this setting removes the sentence, it does not hide the app.

When data leaves the device

There are two ways your health data leaves your phone:

  1. You press a button: "Create backup" or "Sync now".
  2. You turn on the "Automatic sync" switch. This switch is off by default. If you turn it on, a send is triggered in five cases: when you turn the switch on, when you sign in to your account, when you bring the app to the foreground, when you change a record, and when you send the app to the background with a pending change outstanding.

Nothing is sent while the app is closed. There is no background task, no periodic job and no scheduled send. Every send is the continuation of something you just did.

Android's automatic backup

Android backs up app data to the user's Google Drive account by default. This app is excluded from that backup. Your period records, cycle settings, pregnancy details and the contents of the home screen widget are not copied to Google Drive — they are included neither in the cloud backup nor in the device-to-device transfer performed when you move to a new phone.

So the two ways above remain the only ways your health data leaves the device. There is no other.

There is a cost to this: if you change phones. When you move to a new phone, or delete and reinstall the app, your records will not come back by themselves. If you want to move your history, take a backup to your own account from inside the app first — here is how.

The flow, symptoms and mood you record for a day follow the same rule: they stay on your phone and reach your account only by one of the two ways above. The app makes no comment about these records, does not compare them with other days, and says nothing about what is normal.

Data that is not collected

  • No ads. No advertising is shown in the app.
  • No analytics. Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics and any other usage analysis tool are not used.
  • No crash reporting. There is no Crashlytics or similar tool.
  • The Advertising ID is not read.
  • There is no third-party tracking.
  • Location, contacts, camera, microphone and your files are not accessed.

Your data is not sold, not shared for marketing purposes and not used to show advertising. Your health data is under no circumstances used for recruitment or insurance eligibility assessment.

Service provider and where data is stored

Google's Firebase services are used for the account and backup features: Firebase Authentication for sign-in, Cloud Firestore for the backup. Google acts as a data processor in this respect. No other Firebase product (Analytics, Crashlytics, Messaging, Storage, Functions) is used.

Your backups are stored in Cloud Firestore's eur3 multi-region, on Google servers inside the European Union.

Your backup is held under your account alone, in a single document. The server rules permit only your own account to read and write that document; no other user can reach it.

Security

All communication between the device and the server is encrypted with HTTPS/TLS. The data on your phone is kept in app-specific storage that the operating system isolates from other apps.

No raw health data is written to the app's logs: only general event names from a closed list are written; no date, record or avatar information is written.

Retention

Your data is kept until you delete it. It is not deleted automatically after a set period, because it is your own history.

What happens when you delete, and how to do it, is described on the Account and data deletion page.
